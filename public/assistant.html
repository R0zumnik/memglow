Assistant

The AI only proposes. memglow checks the proposal (nothing lost, same folder and group, no note replaced or deleted), shows you every change, and writes only what you approve — after a backup, with Undo. Pick a note to split, or an Organisation suggestion to regroup, with “Do it with …” in Memory cost. Protected groups (Settings) are never crossed.
