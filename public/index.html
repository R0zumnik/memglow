What are your big themes?

These are the big groups of your memory, found in your folders and in the “theme:” key of your notes. Tick the ones to protect: memglow's assistant will never move a note into or out of a protected group, never create a note in another group and never change their “theme” line. You can also rename how a group is shown — your notes are not changed. Change this later in Settings → Protected groups.
